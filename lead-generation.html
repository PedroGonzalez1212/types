FAQs

What's the difference between lead generation and prospecting?
Lead generation attracts the right audience to your business through content, visibility, and trust-building. Prospecting takes a proactive approach by identifying ideal buyers and reaching out directly to start conversations and create opportunities. The strongest growth strategies combine both approaches: one builds awareness and interest at scale, while the other creates direct opportunities with high-value accounts.
Why aren't referrals enough anymore?
Referrals are valuable, but they are difficult to control and scale. While great service helps generate repeat business and recommendations, relying solely on word-of-mouth limits your growth to your existing network. A predictable growth strategy requires additional channels that consistently introduce your business to new audiences and generate qualified opportunities.
How long does it take to generate qualified opportunities?
Generating qualified opportunities in B2B takes time. On average, buyers require approximately 211 days (7 months) and around 76 touchpoints before making a purchase decision. Demand generation helps build the awareness, trust, and credibility needed throughout that journey, ensuring your company is remembered when the buyer is ready to act.
How do we know if we're attracting the right leads?
The right leads are not measured by traffic, clicks, or social media engagement. They are measured by how closely they match your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP). The key questions are whether they come from the right industries, fit your target company profile, and have the challenges you are designed to solve. Ultimately, success is measured by how many of those opportunities become real clients.
