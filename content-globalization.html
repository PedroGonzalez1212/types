FAQs

Why does a B2B company need translation or localization at all?
Nearly 60% of the B2B buying decision process happens before anyone talks to your sales team. The buyer researches, compares and shortlists vendors on their own. Translation and localization exist so that process doesn't quietly work against if people can't access your value offer in their own language.
What is content globalization, and does it actually pay off for a B2B company?
Content globalization is the process of making your brand's content work in every market you sell to, ensuring it's relevant, culturally accurate and usable by everyone. It pays off when the markets you're localizing represent a real pipeline by removing a friction that quietly costs you deals: buyers who cannot fully understand your offer in their own language tend to choose one they can.
Why would a marketing company handle localization and accessibility?
Because localization and accessibility decide whether your marketing reaches the people you are paying to reach. And to help you achieve that goal, Types works alongside The Typesetter, an ISO-certified company that already runs multilingual content and accessibility work for international companies and translation agencies around the world.
Isn't it enough to just translate with AI?
For some content, yes but for content that represents your brand or carries legal or commercial risk, no. AI translation is useful for internal material, support content and first drafts. It is not reliable on its own for websites, campaigns, contracts or anything a buyer uses to decide whether to trust you.
