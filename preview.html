FAQs

How can I tell if my marketing is actually working?
At Types, we believe the ultimate marketing metric is sales. Followers, aesthetics, and engagement only matter if they contribute to measurable business results. That’s what we aim to impact.
Why do marketing agencies often fail to deliver results?
Because they often optimize for visibility instead of business outcomes. At Types, we focus on the metrics, processes, and clarity businesses need to make smarter decisions and turn marketing into measurable results.
How much should a B2B company invest in marketing?
There’s no universal percentage. Start with your commercial goals, understand what it currently costs to reach them, and build your marketing investment around those numbers. Do not focus on someone else’s benchmark.
What should I measure to evaluate marketing ROI?
Keep it simple: define your business goals, measure the results, and understand what those results cost. At Types, we connect marketing performance to the numbers that matter to your business.
